Wind Born

One lake, one bird, one game — built in public, essay by essay. Water that splashes, wind you can ride, a world that keeps time.

Start with the water → Play Fish Hawk Browse the seasons

This site builds a game called Fish Hawk — you are an osprey on a mountain lake, and the dive is real: the splash is a fluid simulation, the wind in your ears is synthesized from your airspeed, and the fish told you where they were. Every essay adds one working piece, every figure runs live on your GPU, and at the end of every season the pieces snap together into something you can play.
